**Headline:** 3 P0, 1 P1, the rest P2 / not-a-bug / already fixed / being built. The four P0/P1 share one theme: the product was only ever run against the fake daemon or a single-human SQLite store, and the two spec'd modes beyond that (paired-local on a shared Postgres; the Android release artefact; a high-risk confirm from the desktop app) fail on the real composition.
